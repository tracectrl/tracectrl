## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Authoritative, precise, security-native. Not breathless AI hype. Practitioner credibility.

**Always:**
- Lead with the problem (98% of enterprises have no AI governance)
- Pair technical depth with business outcomes
- Use developer-native language (trace, telemetry, OTEL, attack graph) — it builds trust with technical buyers
- Emphasise the two pillars: Trace (you can see everything) + Control (you can stop anything)
- Reference TAGAAI and LVD as the differentiators — always spell out on first use
- Tie to enterprise proof: 12 years security DNA, 8 countries, government trust

**Avoid:**
- Generic "AI security" positioning — TraceCtrl is specifically for agentic AI / autonomous agents
- Three-pillar See/Secure/Govern framing (retired)
- "AI guardrails" — that's a different category (LLM output filters, not observability)
- "ASPM" — TraceCtrl is Security Observability & Control, not Application Security Posture Management
- TraceCtrl Discover references in near-term materials — it is deferred roadmap only
- Sub-product names without "TraceCtrl" prefix (e.g., "Guard" alone — must be "TraceCtrl Guard")
