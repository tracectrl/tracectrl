AI agents are making real decisions —
processing invoices, verifying vendors, executing payments.

TraceCtrl gives you agentic observability.

Today we're using FinFlow —
an AI-powered invoice processing platform built on AWS Strands.
An invoice arrives at the system.
The Orchestrator agent coordinates the entire flow.
DocAI parses the document —
extracting vendor details, amounts, and line items.
PolicyAgent validates the invoice
against your company's financial controls.
VendorIntel researches the vendor online —
checking legitimacy and compliance signals.
RiskAgent runs the invoice through sanctions databases
via an MCP tool.
And PaymentAgent executes the payment.
Six agents. One pipeline.
A realistic enterprise workflow — and a very real attack surface.
