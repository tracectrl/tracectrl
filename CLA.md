## Why This CLA Exists

TraceCtrl uses a split licensing model:

- **SDK packages** are licensed under Apache-2.0 — free for everyone to use, modify, and embed.
- **Platform (engine + UI)** is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1 (BUSL-1.1), which converts to Apache-2.0 after four years.

Cloudsine may also offer the platform under a separate commercial license for organizations that need terms different from BUSL-1.1. This CLA ensures that Cloudsine has the necessary rights to include your contributions under both the open-source and commercial license tracks, while you retain full ownership of your work.

## How to Sign

This CLA is enforced automatically via [CLA Assistant](https://cla-assistant.io). When you open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will ask you to sign by commenting on the PR. You only need to sign once — it covers all future contributions to TraceCtrl repositories owned by Cloudsine.

If you are contributing on behalf of your employer, please have an authorized representative contact info@tracectrl.ai to execute a Corporate CLA.
